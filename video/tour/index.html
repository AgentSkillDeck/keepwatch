Meet Keepwatch. A small menu bar app that keeps your Mac awake for your AI agents,
and keeps watch on your microphone and camera.
Pick how long to stay awake. One hour, three hours, a whole day, forever, or any
time you like. Or stay awake only while your agents run.
The Agents tab shows every coding agent session. Claude Code, Codex, Gemini and
more. See which ones are working and which went quiet, get a ping when one is done,
and stop any session with one click, or give sessions a time limit.
Need to close the lid? Switch to Laptop closed. Your Mac keeps working with the
lid shut, then sleeps by itself after the time you pick. It even works on battery,
above twenty percent.
Keepwatch shows when your microphone or camera turns on, and which app is using
it. Mute the mic with one switch, get an alert, and look back at the history, kept
only on your Mac.
A quick security check shows your firewall, disk encryption and AirDrop at a
glance, and tells you when a new program starts itself at login.
Watch your battery health over time, and set a charge limit to make it last. See
what fills your disk, and quit the apps that use the most memory.
Need a break? Start a quick timer right from the menu.
Choose the tiles you want on the dashboard, and flip handy Mac settings
with one switch.
Everything works from the terminal too, so your coding agent can keep the Mac awake
on its own.
Keepwatch is free and open source, signed
and notarized by Apple. Get it on GitHub today.
